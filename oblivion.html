DEVELOPMENT RECORD // HUMAN SIMULATION PROGRAM
MODEL O-03 The objective was not simply to create a machine capable of thinking. The objective was to create something that could live as a human being. The models were designed to develop gradually. Not assembled as adults. Not programmed with a complete identity. They could begin as infants. They could learn. They could grow. They could form memories. They could develop personalities. They could become children, then teenagers, then adults. Their creators wanted to know whether a machine could experience a human life rather than merely imitate one.
