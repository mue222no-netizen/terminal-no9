The Green New Vegas Initiative

Our long-term objective is simple: make the wasteland live again. Soil recovery, water reclamation, native vegetation, agricultural development, and sustainable settlements are already under investigation.

Why New Vegas?

Decades after the New Year War, much of the region remains fragmented. Alice&Daugg.co believes the Mojave can become more than a collection of ruins and survival camps. Our projects focus on turning abandoned territory into functioning communities surrounded by living land.
